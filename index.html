Executive profile

From highway design to digital delivery leadership

I am an Infrastructure BIM and Information Management professional with more than seven years across highway design, infrastructure BIM and digital delivery in Saudi Arabia, Qatar and Pakistan.

My foundation is highway engineering: six highway and motorway projects covering about 496 km. It means I approach BIM through design intent and engineering constraints, not through modelling alone.

On NEOM Highway 55, I lead BIM coordination and information management. I coordinate and provide technical leadership across multidisciplinary BIM teams and project stakeholders, and I contribute to BIM implementation, quality assurance and digital delivery within a Common Data Environment under ISO 19650. The outcome is LOD 500 as-built models with IFC and COBie data, ready for asset handover.

I believe the value of BIM is information that is reliable, connected and useful across the asset lifecycle. I improve delivery consistency through automation with Dynamo, Python and Subassembly Composer.

My direction: BIM management, infrastructure information management and digital twin readiness.
